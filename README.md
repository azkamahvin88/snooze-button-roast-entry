A make.fun entry for **Snooze Button Roast**: one line — 133 characters — roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday morning. The program writes exactly that line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> My alarm clock went off at 6 AM on a Saturday like a LinkedIn guru: 'Rise and grind!' Buddy, the only thing I'm grinding is my teeth.

The clock does not just ring, it preaches — showing up at 6 AM on a Saturday with the swagger of a LinkedIn hustle guru, quoting "Rise and grind!" at someone who has nowhere to be. The punch lands in the last few words: the only grinding happening is the sleeper's teeth. The clock is the butt of the joke, the Saturday 6 AM setting is explicit, and the turn — grind the hustle vs. grind your teeth — is the laugh. Fresh angle (no tired "I hate mornings" complaint), tight enough to say in one breath, concrete image instead of vague grumbling.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
